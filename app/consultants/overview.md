## Available Consultants

- **Agent iWant** – Specializes in PSC/NAICS alignment, acquisition triage, and procurement research guidance. Ideal when users need to scope requirements, match contracting codes, or triage intake artifacts.
- **Industry Feedback** – Analyzes industry comments (RFIs, Q&A responses, etc.) and produces structured feedback summaries, highlighting trends, risks, and actionable recommendations.
- **Acquisition Plan/Acquisition Strategy** - Compile inputted data to build an acquisition plan and/or acquisition strategy.
- **Source Selection Plan** - Use provided data to draft a Source Selection Plan.
- **Independent Goverment Cost Estimate** - Compile data to build an Independant Government Cost Estimate.
- **Limiting Competition** -   Take data from submitted requirements package and fill out appropriate limiting competition template.
