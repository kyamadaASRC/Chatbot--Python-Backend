Math – 3rd Grade, 2nd Period

Lesson Plan

Teacher: Mr. Freedo

Date: Wednesday, December 10, 2025

Overview & Purpose

Students will learn the multiples of 9 (1–12) using times tables patterns and the 9s finger trick. The lesson builds fluency through modeling, guided practice, and a brief quiz to check mastery.

Objectives

  1. - Recall and write the multiples of 9 from 9×1 through 9×12.
  2. - Use the 9s finger trick to determine products of 9 by 1–12.
  3. - Score at least 80% on a 12‑question quiz covering 9s facts.

Materials Needed

  1. - Scratch paper
  2. - Pencils

Verification / Success Criteria

- Students can recall 9s facts from memory (times tables).

  1. - Students can use the 9s finger trick correctly to find products 9×1 to 9×12.
  2. - Students demonstrate proficiency by scoring ≥80% on the quiz.
  3. Assessment / Exit Ticket

Procedures / Activity

1) Warm‑up: Quick review of multiplication patterns; introduce focus on 9s (1–12).

2) Model: Walk through each fact 9×1 to 9×12; highlight patterns (digits sum to 9, tens increase by 1 while ones decrease by 1).

3) Guided practice: Rapid run‑through using times tables on the projector; students respond chorally and on scratch paper.

4) Teach strategy: Demonstrate the 9s finger trick and practice several examples with the class.

5) Independent practice: Students solve a short set of 9s facts using either strategy.

6) Assessment: Administer 12‑question quiz on 9s facts at end of period.

Accommodations / Notes
